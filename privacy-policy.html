Privacy Policy

PureWord: Daily Guess · Developer: Orca Studios
Effective date: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game PureWord: Daily Guess ("the app", "we") handles when you play, and the choices you have. The short version: the app has no accounts and no servers of its own, your game progress stays on your device, and the only third party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the advertisements.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (level, statistics, streaks, settings and unfinished games) is saved only in the app's local storage on your own device. It is never uploaded, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, permanently deletes this data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or location, and contains no login or registration.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To select, deliver, measure and secure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may automatically collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and similar device identifiers;
  • IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location may be inferred;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • ad interaction data, for example which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • diagnostic data about ad performance and crashes of the ad components.

This data is processed by Google and its certified advertising partners according to their own policies. We do not receive it, do not store it, and do not sell it. Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from ads
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob ad technology providers

3. Your advertising choices

  • European Economic Area / United Kingdom: on first launch you are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) where you decide how your data may be used for advertising. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
  • All users: you can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your phone's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones), which limits ad personalisation.

4. Children

The app is a general-audience word game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the advertising components, please contact us and we will point you to the appropriate Google removal process.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade any user data. The only data flow to third parties is the automatic advertising data described in section 2, which goes directly from your device to Google.

6. Security and retention

Ad-related data is transmitted to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We ourselves retain no user data: everything the app creates lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or reset your progress. Data held by Google is retained according to Google's retention policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or similar laws elsewhere), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data. Because the advertising data described above is processed by Google, such requests are best directed through Google's My Ad Center and the in-app consent controls. For anything else, contact us using the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app's store listing notes.
